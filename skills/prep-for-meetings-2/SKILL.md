# prep-for-meetings-2

Produces a tight pre-meeting brief (TL;DR Must Do Before, Must Know, Watch Out, Personal Stakes, Danger Zones, and 30-Second Glance Card).

## Workflow

1. Start from the agenda and the outcome the user wants. Retrieve relevant preparation context, then confirm any sensitivity, time limit or contentious decision.
2. Draft Must Do Before, Must Know, Watch Out, Personal Stakes and Danger Zones. Keep sensitive notes clearly separated from material suitable for sharing.
3. Pair each difficult issue with one question or response the user can use. Label open evidence and possible tradeoffs.
4. Provide a 30-second card and the next preparation action. Keep facts, interpretations and suggested language distinguishable.
