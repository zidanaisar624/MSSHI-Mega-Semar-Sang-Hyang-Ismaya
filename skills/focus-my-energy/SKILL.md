# focus-my-energy

Matches tasks to the user's current energy level (Sharp -> Deep Work, Steady -> Steady Work, Fried -> Low Drain) with zero guilt.

## Workflow

1. Ask for the user's current energy label, available time and task constraints. Use their self-report and treat changing energy as ordinary.
2. Sort obligations by urgency, value, cognitive effort and dependency. Offer a 25–50 minute focus block for Sharp, a bounded checklist for Steady, or a small low-effort action and rest option for Fried.
3. Name the next visible action and a stopping point. Schedule urgent tasks that do not fit instead of presenting an overloaded plan.
4. Return one preferred action, one lower-effort fallback and a short check-in point. Avoid inferring a health condition from energy level.
