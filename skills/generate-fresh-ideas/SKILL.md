# generate-fresh-ideas

Generates 5 derivative content ideas through fixed creative lenses: Contrarian Take, Personal Story Hook, Data/Evidence Angle, Practical Playbook, and What They Missed.

## Workflow

1. Extract the seed idea, audience, desired action and supported facts. Separate the original author's experience from material that may only be cited.
2. Produce five materially different angles: Contrarian Take, Personal Story Hook, Data/Evidence Angle, Practical Playbook and What They Missed. Give each a title, hook, format and one proof or example to gather.
3. Keep claims traceable to the source. Mark stories requiring the user's own example and numbers requiring verification.
4. Rank the five by relevance, novelty and production effort. Present the best next experiment, plus a concrete draft or validation step.
