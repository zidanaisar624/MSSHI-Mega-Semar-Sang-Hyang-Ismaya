# prep-for-meetings

Scans workspace notes, tasks, and past meeting records to produce a TL;DR brief (Must Do, Must Know, Watch Out) and 30-second glance card.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the meeting goal, time, attendees and desired decisions. Read only the relevant notes and task records the user has authorized.
2. Separate confirmed status, unresolved questions and obligations. Identify the owner, deadline and source for each consequential item.
3. Produce Must Do, Must Know and Watch Out. Include decisions to ask for, a suggested agenda if needed and missing information worth resolving.
4. Finish with a 30-second glance card covering meeting goal, strongest evidence, top risk and desired outcome. Mark assumptions explicitly.
